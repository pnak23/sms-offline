### What the real-shell test (`e2e-changes.mjs`) shows
* **Layout**: at 1200×650, 1366×768, 1920×1080 and maximised the page starts at the left edge of the content area and fills it; a form page keeps 960 px from the left; no sideways overflow.
* **Identity**: the first screen before any account, the sign-in screen, the sidebar (and its folded form), the About page, the window title, `backend.log` and `desktop.log` all show the values of `release.json`; the schema number is shown apart from the version.
* **Readiness**: counts of the five states match the shipped list; signing off asks first, records who and when, can be withdrawn, appears in the audit trail in Khmer; the notices on the grading, national-header, fee-currency, attendance-session and report-card pages appear and disappear with the sign-off.
* **Attendance sessions**: a year starts with the whole day and no times; a start without an end, or an end before the start, is refused next to the field; sessions with times start "not confirmed"; confirming records who and when; changing the times takes the confirmation back; the attendance screen shows the sessions as buttons in order, ticks the ones already taken, offers to copy from another session of the day, remembers the last one (in memory); renaming a session leaves the sheet already taken with its old name and times (and the screen says so), and the history shows the old name; a used session has no delete button, an unused one is deleted after a question; the last enabled session cannot be switched off; a switched-off session with a sheet today is still offered (marked off); with one session the choice disappears.
* **Report card**: the form starts as the old card and cannot be saved until changed; the preview follows each choice; the printed cards follow the saved choices (photo frame with a real picture, three signature boxes, the closing note, the national header); with everything off the date and the "draft rules" line remain; subjects can be listed by Khmer alphabet; "back to the defaults" fills the form and, saved, gives today's card again.
* **The worst case on paper**: national header + photo + three signature boxes + closing note + rank + attendance + comment + **twelve real subjects**. As first built it was **1214 px tall (the page holds 1122): it would have spilled onto a second page.** The card now measures itself and sets a tighter type when it would not fit: 950 px in that case. The same decision is made by the preview in Settings (with its "preview with 12 subjects" option), which then says so.

## Things the tests found while this was built (all fixed)
1. The worst-case card overflowed A4 (above) → the card compacts itself.
2. `e2e-app` check 7a failed: "remember the last session" had used `localStorage`, which the program forbids → moved to an in-memory store cleared on sign-out.
3. The first unpacked build failed (`release is not defined` in `package.mjs`) and my own wrapper hid the exit code; the Windows file properties then showed Electron's company "GitHub, Inc." because electron-builder reads the company from `author.name` → `author` is now an object. Both were found only by reading the real output and the real file properties.
4. A small bug in my own option parser (an empty signature count read as 0 boxes) was found by a unit test.
5. `scripts/test-backup-flow.mjs` failed (500 creating an academic year): it deliberately starts the new program on a database one schema behind to rehearse an upgrade, and the new code needs the V7 table to give a year its first session. That state cannot exist in real use (Flyway always migrates to the latest), so the script now writes its two years straight into the old-schema database, as the old program would have left them; the upgrade backup, the restore and the rest then pass. This is a test change, not an app change.
6. The V7 migration test shows what happens to old data: a student marked in two classes on the same day (possible after a class change) keeps the newer record, and the other is written to the audit trail, instead of stopping the upgrade.

## Fourth round (2026-11-04): the roll of honour (តារាងកិត្តិយស)
The owner sent a sample certificate and asked for a printable roll of the top students with at least ten choices. I proposed an 11-item decision list (with the reasons) and the owner answered "approved" after asking for **generated artwork** and **at least 10 options**; I read that as ten artwork styles, and kept a choice of 5 or 10 names as well. **Phase 6 was not started.** Nothing is committed. The roll itself is worked out when it is printed from the ranks the report cards already use. The first version needed no backend change; the **saved default** added later the same day did: new `honor.*` settings keys, validated by the backend, **no migration** (the settings table is key/value).

**Built:**
* **A page of its own** (`/honor-roll`: a sidebar entry "តារាងកិត្តិយស", and a button on the Report cards page that opens it with the examination and class already chosen). On it: the class and the examination, the **date** (the one thing that normally changes), 5 or 10 names, one of **ten artwork styles** (small pictures), the school's five texts with their font style (below), "every class of the year" (one page per class), a **live preview** of the real sheet, and the buttons "Preview and print" (opens `/print/honor-roll`), "Save as the default" and "Back to the standard". **Back** from the preview returns to this page with everything that was chosen (it travels in the address, never in the browser), not to the list of students. This replaces the first version's window (modal) on the Report cards page and the separate settings page.
* **Who is on it:** the students whose backend rank is within 5 (or 10), best first, tied students in Khmer name order. A tie at the cut-off is never cut (a top 5 with two students on rank 5 lists six names, and the page says so in small type); a student with an incomplete result has no rank and is never listed; a short class gives a short list with no empty frames.
* **Layout:** the first name alone on top, then pairs, and a last name alone when the rest is odd (5 → 1,2,2; 10 → 1,2,2,2,2,1). The page is the 180 × 265 mm printable area inside the 15 mm margin; it is laid out with CSS, so a line that is left out (national header, office line, lunar date) gives its room back, the frames are sized from the room that is left, and a long name shrinks to its frame (below half size it wraps).
* **Artwork:** ten original styles drawn by code (`utils/honorArt.ts`): classic gold filigree (closest to the sample), lotus, Angkor towers, stars and ribbon, royal red and gold, laurel, blue guilloche, kids' rainbow, simple line (saves ink) and rising sun. Each gives the frame around a name, the page border and corners, and the flourish under the header. No image file, no download, no licensed artwork.
* **The national header (ព្រះរាជាណាចក្រកម្ពុជា / ជាតិ សាសនា ព្រះមហាក្សត្រ) is always the first thing on the page, in every style, with that style's flourish under it** (the owner's request, made after seeing the first pictures, which had no header). It is printed whatever the "national header" switch of Settings → Printed reports says, because that switch (off by default) is for the report cards and reports; both lines are set in the title font, as in the picture the owner sent. The words come from those settings (the defaults are exactly the owner's two lines); if both lines were emptied the roll uses the standard two. Their official-document check is still pending. The school's name, the class, the homeroom teacher and the director come from what is already entered; Khmer digits follow the existing switch. The program does **not** compute the Khmer lunar date (it cannot be checked offline); the school types it.
* **One saved default, in the school's own words.** The owner sent a picture of their sample with four red boxes and said these are the places to set as the default, and that the user can still choose the date. My first version had only saved a subtitle, an office line, a place and a lunar date, and **fixed several words inside the program** (the two signature titles, the school and class lines, "ត្រូវនឹង"), so the school could not change them. Now Settings → Roll of honour (sidebar entry, with a live preview of the real sheet) saves the number of names, the artwork, "every class" and **five texts, one for each red box**: the block at the top left, the line under the title, the date lines at the bottom right, the class teacher's title, the director's title. **Every word of them is the school's**; the program prints nothing of its own on the certificate except the national header, the title, the artwork, the names and the two person names (the class's homeroom teacher and the director from the school profile).
* **The date drives the words that depend on it.** Words in braces are filled in for each page: {school}, {class} (right on every page of "every class"), {exam}, {date} (the date picked when printing, in its Khmer long form), {month} and {year} of that date. Clicking one of the six words puts it where the cursor is. So choosing only another date changes the month in "លទ្ធផលប្រចាំខែ{month}", the year and the date line by itself. The Khmer **lunar** date has no word: it cannot be computed offline, so the school types it into the date lines (and, because it changes every month, usually types it at printing, not in the saved default).
* **Text keeps its spaces and line breaks.** The boxes keep spaces and line breaks exactly as typed (up to 4 lines and 300 characters each, the same limits in the program and in the backend, which refuses more with a field error) and the printed page shows them the same way (`white-space: pre-wrap`; Windows line ends become one break, only the two ends are trimmed); a box saved empty prints nothing. The boxes warn about a fifth line or the 301st character instead of cutting silently. The page starts from the saved default and the date always starts as today; anything can still be changed for one printing without touching the default. The standard wording (what is printed until the school types its own) is also the backend's default and a test keeps the two equal. This **replaces** the first version's memory-only "last choice" (`stores/honorRollChoice.ts` was deleted).
* **A font toolbar on every box** (the owner asked for "font style or a text editor"): above each of the five boxes, the font (the program's text font, or its title font), the size (small, medium, large, extra large), bold, italic, underline, alignment (left, centre, right) and "keep each line on one line", which makes a long line **shrink** (down to half size) instead of wrapping. It looks like a small editor's toolbar and applies to the whole box; the preview and the printed page follow it at once. It is saved with the default (`honor.formats`, a small JSON text of only what differs from the standard look, validated field by field by the backend). The standard look is the old one: the subtitle in the title font, everything else in the text font, all centred; only the date lines have "keep on one line" on.
* **The date lines are wider.** The owner's picture showed the lunar date wrapping ("ពស / ២៥៧០") because the right column had half the page. It now has three fifths (and the school's column two fifths), and with "keep each line on one line" the long lunar line stays on ONE line (the date lines are 2 lines in all, not 3), shrinking only if it must.
* **Matched to the owner's real sample, by measurement.** The owner put a picture of their real sample next to mine, with the title and subtitle boxed: "it is not the same". Instead of judging by eye I measured both pictures (the bands of ink as fractions of the page) and fixed what differed: the air under the flourish (mine had almost none, the sample about 4 % of the page height), the gap between the title and the subtitle (1.4 %), the subtitle size (mine was 11 % too small, now 12.3 pt), the text colour (the sample is plain black with a gold title; mine was dark brown), the line spacing of the date block and the room above the names for signing (the sample about 28 mm, mine 12), frames a little wider (smaller side margins), the two signature titles bold. After the change the title's top accent, the title, the subtitle's top and width, the date block's top and its width agree with the sample within a fraction of a percent of the page.

**Built (V8):**
* **The catalogue:** `subjects.parent_id` (a main subject has none; a sub-subject points at its main subject; two levels, two triggers keep it true even for a statement that bypasses the service). Settings → Academic lists sub-subjects indented under their main subject and adds one from the main subject's own button or from the general form; a name is unique among siblings; a new sub-subject is offered to the grades its main subject is offered to. **No screen or API moves a sub-subject** (a wrongly placed one is deactivated and created again).
* **Grades:** a sub-subject can only be studied by a grade that studies its main subject (the backend refuses the request with `SUB_WITHOUT_MAIN`; the grade table disables the tick and takes the sub-subjects with a main subject that is switched off).
* **The examination keeps its own copy:** `examination_subjects` stays "the main subjects of the examination" (so the totals can never count a sub-subject on top of its main subject); `examination_sub_subjects` and `sub_subject_scores` are new. A new examination copies every active main subject and its active sub-subjects for each grade, at the same placeholder maximum (100) as before, with a warning on the screen until the first score. The names and the order are the examination's own.
* **Scores:** typed at the lowest level; the score entry lists the sub-subjects of a main subject together under it, and a main subject made of sub-subjects is not offered. The main subject is the **sum** of its sub-subjects (its maximum too, shown read-only); an absent sub-subject counts 0; one not entered makes the main subject, and so the pupil's result, incomplete.
* **Totals, average, percentage and rank count main subjects only.** The results, the monthly ranking list and the roll of honour needed no change.
* **The freeze:** from the first stored score of any kind (`examinations.structure_frozen_at`, set with the grading scale) no subject or sub-subject can be added or removed (`EXAM_STRUCTURE_FROZEN`, also for the rows of other grades that have no score yet); a maximum or weight can still be corrected, as before, not below a score entered.
* **History:** renaming a subject, switching a sub-subject off, narrowing a grade or adding a sub-subject to the catalogue never reaches an examination that exists: its results, report-card data and rows are **byte for byte the same** (proved in `BackendFlowTest` order 63 and on the screen in `e2e-subjects.mjs`); only an examination created afterwards sees the new list.

* **V8 changes nothing already stored:** a migration test builds a V7 school, runs V8 and compares every fact the results are computed from before and after.
